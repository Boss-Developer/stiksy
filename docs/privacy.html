Stiksy Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Published by SF Developers

The short version
  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your photos, stickers, designs and favorites stay on your phone.
  • The app downloads templates and sticker packs from our server, and shows ads from Google AdMob.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

Stiksy ("the app", "we") is a sticker maker for WhatsApp, published by SF Developers. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. If anything is unclear, write to us at sfdevelopers.apps@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The following is created and stored only on your phone. We cannot see it and it is not uploaded to us:

  • Photos you pick from your gallery to make a sticker. They are processed on your phone.
  • Your sticker designs, saved drafts, exported stickers, favorites and the sticker packs you create.
  • Your settings and whether you have removed ads.

You can delete saved designs and downloaded packs under Settings in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings.

3. Information that leaves your device

WhatWhyWho handles it
Requests for templates, categories and sticker packs. Like any internet request, this includes your IP address and basic device and app version details. To download the latest content and check that the app version is supported. Supabase, our hosting provider. The app uses an anonymous session, so no name or email is linked to it.
Advertising identifier, IP address, device and ad interaction data. To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud. Google AdMob, under Google's Privacy Policy.
Purchase of "Remove ads & watermark". To take payment and to restore the purchase on your account. Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that the purchase happened, not your payment details.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services that identify you, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Ads

The free version shows ads, including optional rewarded videos that unlock a premium template or remove the watermark from one sticker. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use the advertising ID on your device. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings under Google > Ads. Buying "Remove ads & watermark" removes ads from the app.

5. WhatsApp and sharing

When you choose "Send to WhatsApp" or "Add to WhatsApp", the app gives your sticker pack to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on your phone, as you asked. What happens next is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. The app also lets you share a sticker through other apps you choose, and save it to your gallery. Nothing is shared unless you take that action.

6. Permissions

  • Internet to download templates and sticker packs and to show ads.
  • Vibrate for small touch feedback.
  • Storage (older Android versions only) to save a sticker to your gallery. On newer versions the app saves through Android's standard media storage.

The app reads photos only through Android's own picker, and only the ones you select.

7. Children

Stiksy is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Content is downloaded over encrypted connections, and downloaded sticker files are checked for integrity before use. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum for that reason.

9. Your choices

  • Delete your designs and downloaded packs in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Contact us with any question about this policy or the data described here.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. Please check back from time to time.
