## Short description (80 max)

## Full description (4000 max)
Make stickers that sound like you. Stiksy turns your photos, emoji and words into WhatsApp stickers, with a little desi flavour built in.

START ANYWHERE
• From a photo in your gallery
• From any emoji on your keyboard
• From your own words, in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English
• From a ready-made template, or a blank canvas

EDIT WITH EASE
• Drag, resize and rotate text, emoji, stickers and photos
• Add as many layers as you like, then reorder, duplicate, lock or hide them
• Beautiful fonts for Urdu, Arabic and English, including Noto Nastaliq Urdu
• Colours, outlines and shadows to make your text pop
• Solid, gradient and photo backgrounds, or keep it transparent
• Undo and redo, and your work saves itself as you go

READY-MADE TEMPLATES
Browse templates for chai time, cricket, Eid, weddings, Urdu calligraphy, funny reactions and more. Pick one, make it yours, send it.

40 DESI STICKER PACKS
Download packs full of Pakistani-inspired stickers: chai and gup shup, desi reactions, and much more. Download once and send anytime, even offline.

SEND IN A TAP
• Add your stickers straight to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business
• Build your own packs from the stickers you make
• Save to your gallery, copy, or share to other apps

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no sign-up. Your photos and designs stay on your phone.

FREE TO USE
Stiksy is free. Free stickers carry a small Stiksy mark, which you can remove for a single sticker by watching a short video. Some premium templates unlock the same way. If you would rather skip all of that, a one-time purchase removes ads and watermarks for good. No subscription, ever.
